0.6.13
- Trading by touch works again: tapping an item no longer closes the trade window
- Touch buttons: an opacity slider (at zero they are hidden but still work) and a switch for their labels
- Subtitle size and dialogue text size sliders in the game settings
- Suggestions can include a save too

0.6.12
- Auto run on a controller: push the stick forward and press LB, pull the stick back to stop
- Problem reports can include a save, so the developer can see where it happened

0.6.11
- The waterline at the sea follows the waves and is softer
- Caustics only show on ground that is under water
- Water seen from above is a little murky, deeper water looks deeper
- Running jumps and slopes no longer cause fall damage
- Jumping at a steep slope slides you down instead of carrying you up it
- Gothic 1: washing diggers no longer show blue squares
- Quicksave keeps 10 rotating slots by default; the mouse wheel browses them in the load menu on PC
- What's new is in the menu and shows once after each update

0.6.10
- Water seen from below: the surface mirrors the bottom, lets the shore show through and draws a waterline when the camera is at the surface
- Under water the view is soft and gently wobbles, and distance fades to lit water instead of black
- Water looks equally clear from above and from below
- Sunlight draws moving caustics on the bottom of shallow water
- Reflections under lily pads no longer streak

0.6.9
- Levels load much faster: the Archolos city takes about 5 seconds instead of 13
- Higher frame rate in big towns: small objects far away no longer cast shadows
- Every tree and plant with wind now sways, and swaying trees keep their collision
- The FPS counter shows the average of the last second
- Support development: a Ko-fi link in the menu
